# Mono single-ended Class-A — first pass

Scope: schematic only, one channel. Original blank schematic saved as ClassA.original.kicad_sch. PCB, project and preferences preserved by SHA-256 comparison. No simulation or bench testing. ERC: zero errors and warnings; 48 connectivity, pin-number and preservation checks pass. Footprints intentionally unassigned pending exact owned package and lead-form verification.

## Circuit and calculations

- QOUT1/QOUT2 are NPN emitter followers. QSINK1/QSINK2 are NPN common-emitter current sinks. Q9 supplies sink base current; Q10 regulates sensed emitter current. There is no complementary signal output branch. The two lower collectors and Q9 collector connect to AMP_OUT, while Q9 base is driven solely by the current regulation circuit.
- Q1/Q2 KSA992 form a PNP differential pair with PNP constant-current tail Q3 and NPN current mirror Q4/Q5. Raising Q1 input voltage reduces its collector current, lowering Q6 base voltage and raising Q6 collector/output voltage. Feedback into Q2 therefore has negative polarity. The active and reference mirror collectors are separate exported nets.
- D1/D2 forward-bias the PNP tail and VAS load. Taking two diode drops of 0.65 V and one PNP VBE of 0.65 V gives tail current 0.65/270 = 2.41 mA and Q7 current 0.65/82 = 7.93 mA. Both are starting estimates marked TUNE. Q6 conducts slightly less than Q7 because of Q8 base current. At idle, VAS collector ~+1.8 V and emitter ~-19.2 V imply ~0.16 W; worst normal swing approaches ~0.3 W. Q7 dissipates ~0.14 W idle and up to ~0.3 W during negative swing.
- Sink emitter-average sensing gives VAVG - VNEG = I x (0.27 + 0.22/2) = 0.38 I, neglecting small sensing and base-current errors. For I = 2 A this is 0.760 V. With Q10 VBE ~0.65 V, required sensing ratio alpha ~0.855. RV1 with R25/R26/R27 spans approximately alpha 0.79–1.0; nominal current ~1.7–2.2 A at VBE 0.65 V. Temperature and transistor spread alter this range: set by measurement after warm-up. With an open wiper, R27 pulls Q10 base to the full sense voltage and reduces current; this is not general fault protection.
- Set R19/R20 individually to 220 mV for 1 A emitter current each. R21 then reads approximately 540 mV at 2 A total. Upper R15/R16 should also read approximately 220 mV unloaded. Power-transistor collector currents differ slightly from emitter currents.
- At 2 A quiescent current, 0.22-ohm ballast resistors dissipate 0.22 W each; upper resistors dissipate about 0.32 W averaged over a 15 W sine wave and ~0.85 W instantaneously at its positive peak. R21 dissipates 1.08 W. Specify non-inductive power resistors, all 5 W. Ballast resistors suppress current hogging but do not guarantee equal sharing; match devices and thermally couple each bank.
- Idle output-stage rail power is ~40 V x 2 A = 80 W. Accounting approximately for ballast/sense drops, upper devices dissipate ~19.8 W each, lower devices ~19.2 W each. Base-drive and front-end power increase total supply demand slightly. Lower devices see approximately 35 V and 1 A each near positive full output; hot DC SOA needs particular attention.
- For 12 W/8 ohms: 13.86 V peak, 1.73 A peak. For 15 W: 15.49 V peak, 1.94 A peak. A 2 A sink gives an ideal resistive Class-A cutoff ceiling of 16 W, before voltage/drive limits. Reactive loudspeakers can demand greater instantaneous current.
- Upper bank current peaks at ~3.94 A. Budgeting beta = 35 gives ~113 mA total output base drive; each 10-ohm stopper drops ~0.56 V. Q8 needs ~1.2 mA base drive using beta = 100. An 8 mA VAS load is adequate for this first pass. Conservative estimates including ~0.65 V CCS emitter drop, ~0.8 V CCS compliance, two follower drops, stopper and ballast drops give positive output headroom around 15.5–16 V at ±20 V. Consequently 12–15 W is plausible, not guaranteed at rail sag or worst-case device parameters.
- Q8/Q9 require thermal margin and external cooling provisions. With output beta = 35, idle driver dissipation can approach 1.1 W each. Q10 dissipates roughly 0.14 W from its 10k bias feed; couple Q10 thermally to the lower-bank heatsink. A warmer Q10 reduces regulated current. Keep Q1/Q2 together and away from power-device heat gradients.
- AC gain = 1 + 19k/1k = 20, or 26.02 dB. C4 makes DC gain approximately unity, reducing amplified input mismatch; feedback corner ~0.34 Hz. Input C1 with 22k gives ~3.29 Hz high-pass response. DNP R2 bypasses C1 for DC input. Matched input bias currents see roughly 22k versus 19k DC source resistance; warm offset below 50 mV is a target, not established.
- C2 100 pF C0G is Miller compensation between Q6 base and collector. C5 10 pF across R30 is DNP. Compensation, base stoppers, current trim/tail/VAS currents and 0.82 uH output inductor require bench confirmation. Feedback is taken before the output inductor. Zobel is 100 nF film plus 10 ohms to GND; inductor has a parallel 10-ohm damping resistor.
- ±20 V rails produce at most approximately 40 V transistor stress in normal operation, below the transistor voltage ratings. Supply electrolytics are correctly polarized and rated 35 V; rail bypasses 50 V. C4 is explicitly bipolar, drawn without a polarity mark. All supply and ground connector pins, diode polarities and test-point nets are checked.

## Review before layout

Review hot DC SOA, case temperature, unequal sharing, startup/shutdown offset and asymmetric rail behavior. Verify closed-loop stability with normal/reactive loads and verify current-sink regulation versus frequency. No relay or DC protection was added. A clean ERC is not a fabrication or bench qualification. No heatsink, chassis, PSU or PCB was designed.
